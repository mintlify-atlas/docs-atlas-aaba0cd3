# Introduction to SEO Machine

SEO Machine is a specialized Claude Code workspace that streamlines the creation of long-form, SEO-optimized blog content for any business. Built on Claude Code, it combines custom commands, specialized AI agents, and Python-based analytics to help you research, write, analyze, and optimize content that ranks well and serves your target audience.

## Key Features

### Advanced SEO Analysis

- **Search Intent Detection**: Automatically classifies content as informational, navigational, transactional, or commercial
- **Keyword Density & Clustering**: Analyzes keyword usage, detects stuffing risks, and identifies topic clusters using TF-IDF
- **Content Length Comparison**: Benchmarks your content against top 10-20 SERP competitors
- **Readability Scoring**: Calculates Flesch Reading Ease, grade level, and sentence complexity
- **SEO Quality Rating**: Comprehensive 0-100 scoring across content, keywords, meta, structure, links, and readability

### Context-Driven Content

All content generation is guided by your business context:

- **Brand Voice**: Define your unique tone, messaging pillars, and writing style
- **Style Guide**: Editorial standards, formatting rules, and terminology preferences
- **SEO Guidelines**: Your specific requirements for keywords, meta elements, and content structure
- **Writing Examples**: 3-5 exemplary posts that teach the AI your style through real examples
- **Internal Links Map**: Catalog of key pages for strategic internal linking
- **Target Keywords**: Organized keyword research by topic cluster

### Workflow Organization

Structured directories keep your content pipeline organized:

```
topics/              → Raw topic ideas
research/            → Research briefs and analysis reports
drafts/              → Work in progress articles
review-required/     → Articles pending review
published/           → Final versions ready to publish
rewrites/            → Updated existing content
landing-pages/       → Landing page content
audits/              → Audit reports
```

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Research">
    Use `/research [topic]` to perform comprehensive keyword research and competitive analysis. The command analyzes top-ranking competitors, identifies content gaps, and creates a detailed research brief.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Write">
    Run `/write [topic]` to generate a 2000-3000+ word SEO-optimized article. The article maintains your brand voice, integrates keywords naturally, and includes internal/external links.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Analyze">
    Specialized agents automatically analyze your content for SEO optimization, meta elements, internal linking opportunities, and keyword placement.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Optimize">
    Use `/optimize [file]` for a final SEO audit before publishing. Get a publishing readiness score and priority fixes.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Publish">
    Optionally publish directly to WordPress using `/publish-draft [file]` with Yoast SEO metadata integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content Quality Standards

Every article produced by SEO Machine meets rigorous standards:

### Content Requirements
- Minimum 2,000 words (2,500-3,000+ preferred)
- Proper H1/H2/H3 hierarchy
- Factually accurate and current information
- Actionable advice for your target audience
- Maintains your brand voice throughout

### SEO Requirements
- Primary keyword density: 1-2%
- Keyword in H1, first 100 words, and 2-3 H2 headings
- 3-5 internal links with descriptive anchor text
- 2-3 external authority links
- Meta title: 50-60 characters
- Meta description: 150-160 characters

### Readability Requirements
- 8th-10th grade reading level
- Average sentence length: 15-20 words
- Paragraphs: 2-4 sentences maximum
- Subheadings every 300-400 words
- Lists and formatting for scannability

## Core Workflows

### Creating New Content

```bash
# Research the topic
/research content marketing strategies

# Review the research brief
# (saved to research/brief-content-marketing-strategies-[date].md)

# Write the article
/write content marketing strategies

# Review agent feedback in drafts/
# - SEO optimization report
# - Meta element options
# - Internal linking suggestions
# - Keyword analysis

# Final optimization pass
/optimize drafts/content-marketing-strategies-[date].md
```

### Updating Existing Content

```bash
# Analyze the existing post
/analyze-existing https://yoursite.com/blog/product-guide

# Review the analysis report
# (check content health score and recommendations)

# Rewrite the content
/rewrite product guide

# Optimize and publish
/optimize rewrites/product-guide-rewrite-[date].md
```

### Creating Landing Pages

```bash
# Research positioning and competitors
/landing-research [topic]

# Create conversion-optimized landing page
/landing-write [topic]

# Audit for CRO issues
/landing-audit [file]

# Publish to WordPress
/landing-publish [file]
```
