The `/research` command performs SEO keyword research and analyzes top-ranking competitor content to create a detailed research brief for article writing.

## Usage

```bash
/research [topic]
```

<ParamField path="topic" type="string" required>
  The topic or keyword to research. Can be a short phrase or full question.
</ParamField>

## Examples

```bash
/research content marketing strategies for B2B SaaS
```

```bash
/research podcast editing software
```

```bash
/research how to grow podcast audience
```

## What It Does

### 1. Keyword Research

- **Primary Keyword**: Identifies main target keyword for the topic
- **Search Volume & Difficulty**: Estimates monthly searches and competition level
- **Keyword Variations**: Finds semantic variations and long-tail opportunities
- **Related Questions**: Discovers what people are asking (People Also Ask, forums, Reddit)
- **Search Intent**: Determines if intent is informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional
- **Topic Cluster**: Identifies how topic fits into your content clusters

### 2. Competitive Analysis

- **Top 10 SERP Review**: Analyzes the top 10 ranking articles
- **Content Length**: Notes word count of top performers (benchmark target)
- **Common Themes**: Identifies topics/sections all top articles cover
- **Content Gaps**: Discovers what's missing from competitor coverage
- **Unique Angles**: Finds underexplored perspectives or insights
- **Featured Snippets**: Identifies snippet opportunities
- **Domain Authority**: Notes which competitors rank

### 3. Context Integration

The command references your context files:

- `context/brand-voice.md` - For messaging fit
- `context/internal-links-map.md` - For related articles
- `context/target-keywords.md` - For priority keywords
- `context/seo-guidelines.md` - For SEO requirements
- `context/features.md` - For product integration opportunities

### 4. Content Planning

- **Recommended Structure**: H2 and H3 outline based on research
- **Content Depth**: Target word count (typically 2000-3000+)
- **Supporting Evidence**: Statistics, studies, or data to include
- **Expert Sources**: Industry experts or quotes to reference
- **Visual Opportunities**: Images, screenshots, or graphics needed
- **Internal Links**: 3-5 key pages to link to
- **External Authority**: 2-3 authoritative external sources

## Output

Creates a comprehensive research brief saved to:

```
research/brief-[topic-slug]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md
```

## Next Steps

After research completion:

1. **Review the brief** - Read through all sections carefully
2. **Run `/write`** - Use the topic to create the article
3. **Reference the brief** - The `/write` command will automatically reference this research

```bash
/write podcast analytics
```

The research brief serves as the foundation for writing optimized, comprehensive content.
